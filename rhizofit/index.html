    What RhizoFit does

    RhizoFit runs the Predictive Ellipse Zoning pipeline of VNStudio on a transverse section of a monocot root with a polyarch stele (maize, rice, sorghum). Every step below is the same Python code as in VNStudio, executed in your browser with Pyodide.

    1. Segmentation. The section, the cell walls (multiscale ridge filter) and the individual cell lumens are extracted. Cortex regions left empty by this first pass are re-examined: once the local brightness gradient is flattened, faint wall networks (typically the pale parenchyma of the inner cortex) are segmented by watershed. Every uncertain region is then checked against the expected cell size at its depth ρ (median of the well-segmented cells of the same ρ band, all around the root): a recovered region larger than twice that size is a cluster of cells whose thin walls were missed and is split accordingly, and an empty region the size of one to three cells is unseen cells rather than aerenchyma. Only larger regions without a wall network are kept as aerenchyma.
    2. Geometric anchor. The large metaxylem vessels (at least five) are detected and an ellipse is fitted around their ring. It fixes the centre, tilt and flattening of the section and defines the coordinate ρ (ρ = 1 on the outer edge of the ring).
    3. Polar unwrap and measured boundaries. The section is unrolled along its tilted ellipses. Along ρ, two profiles are measured without any segmentation: the wall density and the stereological cell size (mean linear intercept). The endodermis/cortex boundary and the inner/outer cortex transition are the change points of these profiles, with a 95 % interval obtained by resampling 16 angular sectors. The pith ends at the inner edge of the vessels; the endodermis and the rhizodermis are one measured cell thick, the rhizodermis following the real outline.
    4. Census and imputation. Cells are counted per tissue. Voids left by tears are filled from the cells facing them across the stele centre (antipodal symmetry); voids empty on both sides of the inner cortex are kept as aerenchyma.

    Limits

    • Monocot roots with a ring of at least five metaxylem vessels only. Without the ring, nothing can be measured.
    • On bright-field micrographs, small stele cells whose walls are not resolved merge and are under-counted.
    • The boundaries move on badly damaged sections: check their 95 % interval in the Metrics tab.
    • This is an exploratory method, validated so far on one section against an expert drawing.

    Pixel size

    Without a pixel size, lengths and areas are given in pixels. With it (µm per pixel, from your microscope calibration), they are given in µm and µm², and cell densities in cells per mm². The samples use an assumed 0.5 µm/px for the micrograph (0.32 µm/px for the drawing, which is a 1.56× enlargement of it).
